Aycash Docs Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Aycash Docs is a document viewer and editor for Android. Your documents stay on your device and in the storage locations you choose. This policy explains the limited usage and crash information the app sends to help us improve it.

What we never collect

  • The contents of your documents, spreadsheets or text files, including anything you type or edit.
  • File names, folder names, file paths or links to your files.
  • Your name, email address, contacts or account details.
  • Your advertising ID. The app has no ads.
  • Your precise location.

What we collect

The app automatically sends the following to Google Firebase (Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics):

  • Usage events: which features are used, for example opening, creating, editing, saving, sharing or searching a document, or zooming a spreadsheet. Each event records only the file type (such as .docx or .csv), a rough size range (such as "1–10 MB"), how long it took to open, and counts (such as how many matches were replaced).
  • Crash and error reports: the type of error and where in the app's code it happened. Error messages are removed before sending, because they can contain text from your files.
  • Device and app information: device model, Android version, app version, language, and an approximate country/region derived from your IP address.
  • An app-instance identifier created by Firebase. It lets us count how many installs use a feature. It is reset if you reinstall the app or clear its data, and it is not linked to your identity.

How we use it

Only to understand which features are used, fix crashes and improve performance. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except Google, which processes it for us as described in the Firebase privacy information.

Retention

Google Analytics keeps event-level usage data for 2 months and then deletes it automatically; only aggregated statistics (for example "how many documents were opened this week") are kept after that. Firebase Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days.

Your choices

Telemetry is always on in the app. If you don't want any usage data sent, please don't install Aycash Docs, or uninstall it. To ask for data linked to your install to be deleted, contact us at the address below.

Children

Aycash Docs is not directed at children under 13.
